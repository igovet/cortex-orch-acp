# Optional senior consultation

Use for one consequential decision with concrete unresolved uncertainty, not routine
work, unchanged packets or confidence alone. It is advisory and does not transfer
incident ownership or create a mandatory stage.

Assign `senior_consultant` through `delegate` with a compact packet: question, outcome, constraints,
verified artifact versions, selected published reports, facts/hypotheses, attempts
and next discriminating check. The consultant reads only these reports and writes
its own report; missing source facts require a precise evidence request, not guesses.
Keep the incident owner active and repeat consultation only for changed evidence.
Use the model/effort policy in [worker routing](worker-routing.md).
